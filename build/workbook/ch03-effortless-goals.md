## Step 1: Surface Goal

Write down what you think you want. Don't edit yourself; just capture the goal as it naturally appears in your mind.

::: {.lines fill=page}
:::

## Step 2: Radical Self Honesty

Why do you want this? Keep asking "why" until the answer feels uncomfortably honest.

::: {.lines fill=page}
:::

What core desire does this fall into? Circle one or write your own:

::: choices
- Security
- Acceptance
- Autonomy
- Competence
:::

::: {.lines n=4}
:::

::: newpage
:::

## Step 3: Create a Goal That Serves Your Desire

Now that you know your underlying desire, write a concrete, actionable goal that serves it directly.

::: {.lines fill=page}
:::

## Step 4: Minimize

What is the smallest version of this goal that would still satisfy your desire? Can it be achieved with one single strategy?

::: {.lines fill=page}
:::
