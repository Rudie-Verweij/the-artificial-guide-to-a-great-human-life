Think of a recent achievement, gain, or milestone. Something you worked for and got. Write it here:

::: {.lines n=3}
:::

## The Audit

What did I expect to feel? Be specific: name the anticipated emotion.

::: {.lines fill=page}
:::

What did I actually feel? Was it what you expected? How long did it last before the baseline reasserted itself?

::: {.lines fill=page}
:::

What did this cost me? Not just money: time, energy, relationships, opportunities foregone.

::: {.lines fill=page}
:::

What new exposure does this create? What do I now have to maintain, protect, or live up to? What can I now lose that I couldn't lose before?

::: {.lines fill=page}
:::

What almost went wrong? What could have derailed this? What did I get away with?

::: {.lines fill=page}
:::

## Patterns

Looking at your answers: what patterns do you notice in how you respond to success? To failure? What does loss teach you that gains don't?
